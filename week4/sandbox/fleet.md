# Electric Vehicle Comparison: Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E

This briefing compares the Tesla Model Y and the Ford Mustang Mach-E as potential candidates for our 100-car sales fleet.

## Tesla Model Y

*   **Range:** Offers an electric range of 260–310 miles, with some trims achieving an EPA estimated 320 miles.
*   **Charging:** Can add up to 160 miles of range in 15 minutes, contributing to lower charging costs.
*   **Cargo Space:** Provides approximately 74 cubic feet of cargo space, suitable for sales materials.

## Ford Mustang Mach-E

*   **Price & Range:** Starting at $37,795, it offers an EPA-estimated range of up to 320 miles.
*   **Charging:** Access to over 84,000 chargers nationwide through the FordPass Charging Network.
*   **Cargo & Tech:** Provides up to 64.4 cubic feet of cargo space and includes a 15.5" touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, and Ford Co-Pilot360 driver-assist technology.

## Comparison

Both vehicles offer comparable impressive ranges, with the Mach-E having a slight edge in starting price. The Model Y boasts more cargo space, which could be beneficial for carrying more sales materials. However, the Mach-E offers a more extensive public charging network through FordPass and integrates popular tech features like wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, which are often valued by fleet drivers.

## Recommendation

For a sales fleet, the **Ford Mustang Mach-E** is recommended. While the Tesla Model Y offers slightly more cargo space, the Mach-E's competitive pricing, extensive charging network access, and integrated driver-assist and infotainment technologies provide a more well-rounded and practical package for sales professionals on the road. The broader charging infrastructure support could significantly reduce range anxiety and streamline operations for a large fleet.
