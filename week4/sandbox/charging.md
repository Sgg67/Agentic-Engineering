# Public EV Charging Landscape in the US

## Overview of Public Charging Infrastructure

The United States currently boasts a significant and growing public electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure. As of recent data, there are over 250,000 public EV charging ports available across more than 80,000 locations nationwide. This includes tens of thousands of DC fast chargers, which are crucial for rapid charging needs, especially for fleet operations.

The number of charging points and locations continues to expand rapidly, driven by increasing EV adoption and governmental initiatives to build out the charging network. This growth provides a progressively robust foundation for fleets transitioning to electric vehicles, ensuring greater accessibility and reducing range anxiety.

## Major Charging Networks for Fleets

For a sales fleet transitioning to electric vehicles, reliable and widespread charging networks are paramount. Two major networks stand out for their extensive coverage and suitability for fleet operations:

### ChargePoint

ChargePoint is one of the largest and most comprehensive EV charging networks in the US. It offers a wide range of charging solutions, from Level 2 AC chargers to DC fast chargers. ChargePoint is particularly well-suited for fleet managers due to its robust networking platform that connects and coordinates the needs of EV drivers and fleet operations. Their services often include tools for managing charging sessions, monitoring energy consumption, and optimizing charging schedules, which are invaluable for efficient fleet management.

### Electrify America

Electrify America has established itself as a leading open hyper-fast charging network across the nation. With a strong focus on high-speed DC fast charging, Electrify America is ideal for fleets that require quick turnarounds and minimal downtime. Its expansive network of fast chargers along major travel corridors and in metropolitan areas ensures that fleet vehicles can be rapidly recharged, making it a critical asset for long-distance routes and busy operational schedules. As an open network, it supports a wide variety of EV models, offering flexibility for diverse fleet compositions.
